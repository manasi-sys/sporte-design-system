# Mobile guidelines

<div style={{ background: "#FEF3C6", border: "1px solid #E17100", borderRadius: 12, padding: "12px 16px", margin: "16px 0" }}>
  **Draft for review.** Figma only defines the desktop layout (1440px), so nothing on this page comes from the Figma file.
  It's built from how the components actually measure today and from platform standards (Apple 44pt, Material 48dp,
  WCAG 2.2). Anything marked **Decision** needs design sign-off before it's built.
</div>

The components, tokens, font (Inter) and icons (Material Symbols) are the same on a phone. What changes is how they're
sized, laid out and touched. "Phone" below means any screen narrower than **640px** (the `sm` breakpoint).

## 1. What changes on a phone

- **Fingers, not a cursor.** There's no hover, and a finger needs a much bigger target than a mouse pointer.
- **One column.** Little room, so content stacks and the main action stays within thumb reach.
- **The phone's own UI.** A notch at the top and a home bar at the bottom can cover content.
- **The user's text size.** People enlarge text in their phone settings, and layouts must cope with it.

## 2. Touch targets

Every tappable thing needs a tap area of at least **44 × 44px** (48px where there's room) with at least **8px** between
neighbours. The tap area can be bigger than what's drawn.

## 3. Typography

- **Inputs use 16px text on a phone** (they're 14px today). Below 16px, iPhone Safari zooms the page in when a field
  is tapped. This one isn't optional for web.
- **Nothing smaller than 12px.** The two 11px caption styles are fine for desktop but too small to rely on a phone.
- **Body text is 16px for reading**; 14px is acceptable only for dense secondary lists. **Decision**
- **Size text in rem, not px**, so it follows the user's text-size setting. The components use px today.
- Inter and the existing weights stay as they are.

## 4. Layout and spacing

- **Page gutters are 16px**, the same as card padding. Use the existing spacing scale (4, 8, 12, 16, 24).
- **Stack everything in one column.** Cards go full width. Stat cards can sit two across at most.
- **Keep clear of the notch and home bar** by adding the device's safe-area padding to top and bottom bars.
- **Put the main action where a thumb reaches**: at the bottom, in a sticky footer, not top-right.

## 5. How each component adapts

- **Sidebar (NavItem)** becomes a **bottom tab bar** for the 3 to 5 main destinations, with a "More" drawer for the
  rest. Collapsible groups become a list screen inside that drawer. **Decision**
- **Tabs** must scroll sideways on a phone. The five-tab example is 797px wide today and **currently overflows a
  phone screen without scrolling**, so this needs building. The active tab should scroll into view.
- **Modal** becomes a **bottom sheet** (slides up, full width, 16px rounded top corners) for short tasks, or goes
  **full screen** for forms. Confirm dialogs stay centred with 16px side gutters. **Decision**
- **Buttons** go full width in forms and footers. When two sit together, stack them with the main one on top.
- **Card** stacks full width. **Tables** become a list of Cards, one per row (there's no table component yet).
- **Inputs** go one per row with the label above (already so). Set the right keyboard (email, phone, number).
  The Select already uses the phone's native picker, which is the right choice.
- **GradientBackground**: its shapes are fixed at 520 × 624px and 390 × 468px, so on a 390px-wide phone each one
  is about as large as the whole screen. Scale them down (about 60%) or hide them on a phone. **Decision**, and it needs building.
- **Badge** is non-interactive and needs no change.

## 6. Touch behaviour

- **No hover.** Never hide information or actions behind it. Wrap hover styles so they only apply on devices that
  have a mouse, otherwise the hover colour (including the blue primary-button hover) can stay stuck after a tap.
- **Pressed state is the touch feedback.** Buttons already have one. Add a pressed style (the blue 10% tint) to nav
  items and list rows. Feedback should show within about 100ms.
- **Keep focus rings.** They still matter for external keyboards and switch controls.
- **Don't make swipe the only way** to do something. Offer a visible button as well.
- **Respect "reduce motion"** for sheet and drawer animations.

## 7. Accessibility on a phone

- **Contrast matters more outdoors.** Avoid putting mid-grey (`grey-600`) small text straight on the gradient, and
  keep the known low-contrast spots (the primary button hover) away from anything critical.
- **Text must survive 200% scaling.** Labels wrap rather than clip, and nothing sits at a fixed height.
- **Screen readers** (VoiceOver, TalkBack) already get proper labels from the components. The Modal uses the
  browser's native dialog, which they support.

## Open decisions

1. Bottom tab bar plus "More" drawer for the sidebar on a phone?
2. Modals as bottom sheets, or full-screen for forms?
3. Body text at 16px on a phone, or keep 14px?
4. Gradient shapes: scale down, or hide on a phone?
5. Build the missing pieces (scrolling Tabs, larger tap rows, 44px close button, hover-only-on-mouse, 16px phone
   inputs)? None of these exist yet.
